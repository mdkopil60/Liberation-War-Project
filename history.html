History of Liberation War

The Great Liberation War of 1971

The Bangladesh Liberation War began on 26 March 1971. The people of Bangladesh fought for independence, freedom and their rights against oppression.

After nine months of struggle and sacrifice, Bangladesh achieved victory on 16 December 1971. This historic day marked the birth of an independent nation.
